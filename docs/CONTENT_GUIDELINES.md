## Source of Truth

The current source is `Sambath_A_R_Resume.pdf` (reviewed 2026-10-16). Both `/resume/Sambath_A_R_Resume.pdf` and the legacy `/resume/Sambath-A-R-IT-Cloud-Infrastructure.pdf` URL serve identical bytes. Download buttons use the preferred-filename alias with `download="Sambath_A_R_Resume.pdf"` and no scripted download handler.

Resume alignment includes tenant-to-tenant and Google Workspace-to-M365 migrations, BitTitan/Cloudiway, Windows Autopilot support, Intune Win32 packaging and Blob Storage. Four tenants and 100+ users describe administration scope, not four migrations. The 99.9% availability figure belongs to VMware operations at SVM Tech. ## Allowed Content

- Verified roles, dates, companies, and locations.
- Verified technologies and operational areas.
- Verified outcomes such as endpoint compliance, manual effort reduction, cost reduction, tenant count, user count, endpoint migration count, Azure resource count, and availability.

## Disallowed Content

- Fabricated metrics.
- Unsupported employer or client claims.
- Fake testimonials.
- Skill ratings, percentages, or progress bars.
- Unsupported certifications.
- Prominent phone number exposure in the website UI.

## V3 Content Constraints

Use `Case Studies` consistently in navigation, headings, and the hero CTA (`View Case Studies`). Preserve the headline, roles, supporting paragraph, employment history, contact information, and resume asset.

The primary impact grid contains seven metrics: 100% endpoint compliance; 40% manual effort reduction; 40% M365/cloud cost reduction; 4 Microsoft 365 tenants managed across multiple business environments; 100–150 endpoints; 10+ Azure VMs/Storage Accounts; and 99.9% availability. The 100+ supported-users fact remains valid but is no longer a primary grid metric. Presentation emphasis and animation must not change the final values or imply greater operational scale.

V3.1 uses the approved cost-optimization problem and approach copy, distinguishes PowerShell/Microsoft Graph user lifecycle automation from license audits/right-sizing, and uses “modern IT environments” in the hero. The Measured Impact title uses “Measured operational impact” to avoid implying independent verification. AI Router context describes request channels, model/workflow selection, and IT support/internal workflows without additional outcome claims.

Architecture connections communicate relationships between verified technology areas, not a claim of a specific deployed tenant topology. AI diagrams describe a modular architecture for task-specific IT support and internal workflows, not a commercial product or autonomous infrastructure operation. Technology relationship highlighting does not imply a deployed integration.
